# Voice Guide

Use this template to define your writing voice. Fill in each section with your own preferences. Delete the prompts once you've written your answers.

## Tone

_How should your writing sound? Think about a conversation you'd have with a specific person in a specific setting. "Explaining something to a smart colleague over coffee" is more useful than "professional but approachable."_



## What good looks like

_Link or paste 2-3 examples of writing you admire (your own or others'). What do they have in common? Name the specific patterns._



## Anti-patterns

_What do you never want to see in your writing? Be specific. "No soapboxing" is better than "keep it professional." Include words, phrases, structures, and habits._



## Topics

_What do you write about? List the 3-5 areas where you have real experience (not aspirational topics)._



## Rhythm and structure

_Any mechanical preferences? Em dash limits, paragraph length, sentence variety, how you handle lists or headings._
